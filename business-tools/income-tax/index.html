UK Income Tax Estimator Calculator

Estimate annual Income Tax on non-savings employment earnings in England, Wales or Northern Ireland for 2026–27.

Enter values to calculate

Annual amount, not monthly pay.

Results

Press Calculate to show your result. Editing an input clears the previous calculation.

Formula and worked example

Standard Personal Allowance is £12,570; above £100,000 it falls £1 per £2, reaching zero at £125,140. On taxable income, charge 20% on the first £37,700, 40% on the next £87,440 and 45% above that.

Example: £40,000 gross annual employment income, using the standard allowance, gives an estimated £5,486 annual Income Tax before National Insurance and other deductions.

Important assumptions

England, Wales and Northern Ireland only; 6 April 2026–5 April 2027, standard personal allowance. NOT applicable to Scotland and NOT a take-home-pay calculation. Excludes National Insurance, pension contributions, student loans, tax-code changes, other reliefs, savings/dividends, benefits and property income. Official source: www.gov.uk/income-tax-rates.

Enter numbers using a full stop for decimals and optional comma thousands separators. VAT amounts use whole pennies.

Your calculations stay in your browser

These tools calculate using the values you enter. Calculator inputs are not intentionally sent to or stored on OASYS servers.

You can download your results to your own device. Choose a secure location if the information is commercially confidential.

Please avoid entering personal, financial-account or other sensitive information that is unnecessary for the calculation.

Results are estimates based on your inputs and stated assumptions. Check them before using them for important business, financial or employment decisions.
