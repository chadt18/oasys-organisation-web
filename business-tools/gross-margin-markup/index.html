Gross Margin and Markup Calculator

Calculate gross profit, gross margin and markup from your sales price and cost. Keep the difference between margin and markup clear.

Enter the figures to calculate

A zero or positive amount. Example: 100

A zero or positive amount. Example: 60

For clarity only. The calculator does not add or remove VAT.

Results

Results appear after you select Calculate. Editing an input clears the previous answer.

Formula and worked example

Gross profit = sale price − cost. Margin = gross profit ÷ sale price × 100. Markup = gross profit ÷ cost × 100.

Example: Selling price £100 and cost £60 gives £40 gross profit, 40% gross margin and approximately 66.67% markup.

Important assumptions

Use the same currency and VAT basis for both figures. Selling price and cost may be VAT-inclusive, VAT-exclusive or unspecified, but VAT is not calculated. Overheads and other expenses are excluded.

Enter numbers using a full stop for decimals. Optional comma thousands separators and a leading £ symbol are accepted. Up to six decimal places and magnitudes up to 1,000,000,000 are supported.

Your calculations stay in your browser

These tools calculate using the values you enter. Calculator inputs are not intentionally sent to or stored on OASYS servers.

You can download your results to your own device. Choose a secure location if the information is commercially confidential.

Please avoid entering personal, financial-account or other sensitive information that is unnecessary for the calculation.

Results are estimates based on your inputs and stated assumptions. Check them before using them for important business, financial or employment decisions.
