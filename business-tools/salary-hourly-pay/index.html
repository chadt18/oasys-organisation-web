Formula and worked example

Annual gross = hourly rate × paid hours per week × paid weeks per year. Hourly gross = annual salary ÷ (paid hours × paid weeks). Monthly gross = annual ÷ 12.

Example: £52,000 per year at 40 paid hours and 52 paid weeks gives £25 per hour. £15/hour at 40 hours for 46 paid weeks gives £27,600 per year.

Important assumptions

Gross estimates only; deductions, pensions, National Insurance, income tax, overtime, holiday entitlement and minimum-wage compliance are not determined. Enter paid hours and paid weeks for the same employment arrangement.

Enter numbers using a full stop for decimals and optional comma thousands separators. VAT amounts use whole pennies.

To estimate annual Income Tax separately, open the Income Tax tool. We do not transfer your figures between calculators.

Your calculations stay in your browser

These tools calculate using the values you enter. Calculator inputs are not intentionally sent to or stored on OASYS servers.

You can download your results to your own device. Choose a secure location if the information is commercially confidential.

Please avoid entering personal, financial-account or other sensitive information that is unnecessary for the calculation.

Results are estimates based on your inputs and stated assumptions. Check them before using them for important business, financial or employment decisions.
