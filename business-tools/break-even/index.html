Formula and worked example

Contribution = selling price per unit − variable cost per unit. Break-even = fixed cost ÷ contribution, rounded up to whole units when contribution is positive.

Example: £1,000 fixed costs, £25 sale price and £15 variable cost gives £10 contribution per unit and 100 break-even units.

Important assumptions

All costs and prices must use the same currency, VAT basis and accounting period. This estimate excludes changing costs, tax and uncertain demand.

Enter numbers using a full stop for decimals. Optional comma thousands separators and a leading £ symbol are accepted. Up to six decimal places and magnitudes up to 1,000,000,000 are supported.

Your calculations stay in your browser

These tools calculate using the values you enter. Calculator inputs are not intentionally sent to or stored on OASYS servers.

You can download your results to your own device. Choose a secure location if the information is commercially confidential.

Please avoid entering personal, financial-account or other sensitive information that is unnecessary for the calculation.

Results are estimates based on your inputs and stated assumptions. Check them before using them for important business, financial or employment decisions.
