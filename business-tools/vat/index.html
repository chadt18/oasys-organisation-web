Results

Press Calculate to show your result. Editing an input clears the previous calculation.

Formula and worked example

Add VAT: VAT = net × rate; gross = net + VAT. Remove VAT: net = gross ÷ (1 + rate); VAT = gross − net.

Example: £100 net + 20% VAT = £120 gross. Removing VAT from £120 gross leaves £100 net and £20 VAT.

Important assumptions

The correct VAT rate depends on the supply. Zero-rated and exempt are different. This tool does not determine VAT treatment or VAT registration obligations. Official source: www.gov.uk/vat-rates.

Enter numbers using a full stop for decimals and optional comma thousands separators. VAT amounts use whole pennies.

Your calculations stay in your browser

You can download your results to your own device. Choose a secure location if the information is commercially confidential.

Results are estimates based on your inputs and stated assumptions. Check them before using them for important business, financial or employment decisions.
