Why OASYS exists

The problem is not a lack of data. It is the effort required to assemble a trustworthy operating picture.

Managers often begin the day moving between commerce platforms, stock systems, finance reports, spreadsheets, campaign dashboards, messages and internal documents. That assembly work is not the manager's highest-value contribution. Their value is judgement: prioritising, challenging assumptions, coordinating people and making accountable decisions. OASYS reduces the assembly burden so that judgement can be applied sooner, with a clearer evidence trail.

How OASYS works

Four clear steps from scattered information to a decision-ready brief.

1

Bring together approved information

Start with the smallest useful set of commercial, cost and operational evidence.

2

Standardise and check freshness

Definitions, dates and source confidence are made explicit before interpretation.

3

Apply defined exception rules

Material conditions are surfaced using evidence-backed rules, with provenance retained.

4

Human decides

The Morning Brief frames attention and evidence. The manager owns the decision.

If evidence is incomplete, stale or contradictory, your OASYS dashboard surfaces the uncertainty rather than inventing an answer.

OASYS Levels 1–5

One dashboard. Five permission levels.

The levels describe how much OASYS is allowed to do — not five different products you have to buy at once. Start with the lowest useful level and increase permission only when the evidence, controls and governance justify it.

1

Brief

OASYS organises the evidence, surfaces exceptions and shows decisions waiting. The human interprets and decides.

Current design-partner offer
2

Recommend

OASYS adds constrained options and recommendations. The human chooses what happens next.

3

Draft

OASYS prepares messages, actions or outputs. Nothing is released without human review.

4

Approval-gated action

OASYS can carry out approved tasks after explicit human sign-off, with an audit trail.

5

Bounded automation

Pre-agreed low-risk tasks can run within defined limits, monitoring and human governance.

Commercial boundary: Level 1 is the only standardised priced offer today. Levels 2–5 show the permission pathway and can be discussed during validation; they are not yet standard commercial packages.
