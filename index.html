How OASYS works

Four clear steps from scattered information to a decision-ready brief.

1

Bring together approved context

Start with the role, decision rights, operating rhythm, current evidence and, where useful, the organisation's targets, budget or forecast.

2

Standardise and check freshness

Definitions, dates, ownership, cadence and source confidence are made explicit before interpretation.

3

Compare plan with reality

OASYS surfaces meaningful variance and defined exceptions while preserving the evidence trail.

4

Human decides

The Morning Brief frames attention and evidence. The manager owns the decision.

OASYS does not need to invent a prediction to be useful. It can compare what the organisation said should happen with what is actually happening. If evidence is incomplete, stale or contradictory, the dashboard surfaces that uncertainty rather than inventing an answer. Important information and actions should remain within three interactions of the main dashboard.

Why OASYS exists

The problem is not a lack of data. It is the effort required to assemble a trustworthy operating picture.

Managers often begin the day moving between commerce platforms, stock systems, finance reports, spreadsheets, campaign dashboards, messages and internal documents. That assembly work is not the manager's highest-value contribution. Their value is judgement: prioritising, challenging assumptions, coordinating people and making accountable decisions. OASYS reduces the assembly burden so that judgement can be applied sooner, with a clearer evidence trail.

OASYS Levels 1–5

Start with visibility. Increase permission as trust is earned.

The levels describe how much OASYS is permitted to do. You do not need five separate products. The same operating layer can move from briefing the manager to bounded automation as the use case, controls and governance mature.

Human-ledBounded autonomy
Level 1Available now

Brief

Know what matters before opening the other systems.

  • Morning Brief
  • Plan vs actual variance
  • Evidence-backed exceptions
  • Source provenance

Human control: the manager interprets the brief, challenges it where needed and makes every decision.

Level 2Recommend

Recommend

See credible options without handing over the decision.

  • Constrained options
  • Ways to respond to material variance
  • Risks and trade-offs

Human control: OASYS can recommend, but the manager selects the option and owns the decision.

Level 3Prepare

Draft

Turn a decision into review-ready work.

  • Draft emails
  • Draft recovery / action updates
  • Prepared actions

Human control: drafts remain private until a person reviews, edits and releases them.

Level 4Approve

Approval-gated action

Let approved routine actions happen without losing control.

  • Explicit approval gate
  • Approved corrective actions
  • Action log and defined permissions

Human control: OASYS cannot execute the action until an authorised person explicitly approves it.

Level 5Automate

Bounded automation

Automate mature, low-risk work inside agreed limits.

  • Pre-agreed low-risk corrective tasks
  • Monitoring against agreed thresholds
  • Escalation rules + manual override

Human control: people set the rules, review performance and retain a manual override to pause or stop automation.
