## Narration script

Think about starting your working day as a commercial manager. Sales figures sit in one place, costs in another, and stock information somewhere else. Before you can decide what needs doing, you've already spent time bringing everything together.

OASYS Organisation is exploring a simpler way to start. The Morning Brief would show what changed, what matters today, the evidence behind it, and which decisions are waiting for you.

Let's take an online shop. Sales might be doing well, but the cost of one popular product has risen, and there may be only a couple of days of stock left. That's the kind of thing a Morning Brief could flag. Instead of another page full of numbers, you'd see the problem, the figures it came from, and the options that need your judgement.

OASYS doesn't decide whether to order more stock, change a promotion or accept the risk. You do. It prepares a clearer picture so you can focus on the decision rather than assembling the information.

It would be built around the role, the organisation's approved sources and the decisions that person is allowed to make. We'd start with a fictional prototype, not access to your live systems.

Our current offer is a design-partner Morning Brief for ecommerce and commercial teams. We're inviting people to take a twenty-minute look at the example and tell us whether it would help with a real part of their day.
