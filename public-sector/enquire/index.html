What happens next?

We'll read what you send.

If it sounds like something we could help with, we'll get back to you and talk it through. We'll also tell you if the software you already have might be the better answer.

How we handle your enquiry

When you choose to send an email, it goes from your email service to the existing OASYS business mailbox. This webpage doesn't save what you type or send it to a website server.

We'll use your name, work email, organisation and description to read and reply to your question, and arrange a conversation if you want one. We won't put you on a marketing mailing list just for getting in touch.

People responsible for OASYS enquiries, and the email services involved, may handle your message. We'll keep it only for as long as needed to manage the conversation and any business record-keeping requirements. We haven't set a fixed retention period for these early enquiries yet.

To ask about your information, correct something or request deletion, use the direct email address above. Please avoid sending residents' details, patient records, passwords or confidential documents.

This is a practical explanation of how the current email enquiry works, not a claim that a full website submission system has been implemented. Formal privacy details must be finalised before any replacement hosted form is launched.
